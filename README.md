# 🛒 Zepto SQL Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Zepto product data using SQL** to explore product categories, stock availability, pricing, discounts, inventory, and other business-related metrics.

The project covers three main areas:

* **Data Exploration**
* **Data Cleaning**
* **Business Insight Queries**

The analysis is performed using SQL on the `zepto` table.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Explore the Zepto dataset and understand its structure
* Identify missing and duplicate product information
* Clean pricing-related data
* Analyze product categories and stock availability
* Calculate category-level revenue and inventory metrics
* Identify products and categories based on discounts, pricing, and weight

---

## 🧹 Data Exploration

The project explores:

* Total number of rows
* Sample product records
* Null values
* Different product categories
* Products that are in stock vs. out of stock
* Products appearing multiple times in the dataset

---

## 🧽 Data Cleaning

The following cleaning steps are performed:

* Identifying products with zero MRP or selling price
* Removing products with zero prices
* Converting prices from **paise to rupees**
* Rounding converted prices to two decimal places

---

## 📊 Business Insights

The project includes SQL queries to analyze:

1. Top 10 products based on selling price
2. High-MRP products that are out of stock
3. Revenue generated by each category
4. Products with MRP above ₹500 and discounts below 10%
5. Top 5 categories by average discount percentage
6. Price per gram for products weighing at least 100g
7. Product weight classification into **Low, Medium, and Bulk**
8. Total inventory weight by category
